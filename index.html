01 · About

About me

My work sits where data meets decisions. Across B2B and small-business software, I've built the measurement frameworks that tell leaders what's really working: A/B, geo and holdout experiments, media mix modeling, multi-touch attribution and incrementality testing.

Today I lead marketing analytics and operations at Squarespace, where I also measure how AI tools change the way teams work: adoption, time saved and the quality of what they produce. That work got me curious about building agents myself, so I'm doing that next, in the open.

I'm still hands-on in SQL, Python and statistical modeling, and I care most about turning complex analysis into a clear recommendation someone can act on.
